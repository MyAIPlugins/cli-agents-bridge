# Rubric: plan "what and why" (part A)

Adapted from google/elephant-goldfish-model (feature-spec and Heilmeier questions), Apache-2.0.

It serves the ESC for part A of the plan in lots with a plan gate, ARC for every proposal, CRI2 to judge them (and SEC, on the risks and limits item). Whoever applies it checks that every point is there and holds up; a missing point is a finding.

1. **Goal**, in your own words and without jargon: what changes for whoever uses the system.
2. **How it works today** and where it stops. Read in the code or in the documents, not remembered.
3. **What is new** in the approach.
4. **Who benefits**, and who pays the cost.
5. **Alternatives**: at least two, evaluated and discarded for concrete technical reasons, including "do nothing" when it is possible. A single path in a lot with a plan gate = NO-GO on the plan.
6. **Risks and limits**: failure modes, security, scale, and the mitigation of each one.
7. **Estimated cost**: time and tokens, marked as an estimate.
8. **Exams**: the intermediate and final criteria, that is the BAR criteria, each one verifiable by a deterministic gate.
9. **Terms**: every new term, acronym or system is defined in the document.

For ARC, add the **recommendation** among the alternatives and, written last, the **strongest counterargument against its own recommendation**.
