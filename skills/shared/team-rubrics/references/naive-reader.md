# Rubric: naive reader

Adapted from google/elephant-goldfish-model (comprehension), Apache-2.0.

It serves CRI2 as a mandatory lens, and whoever reviews ARC's proposals. Read as a member of the team who did not take part in the conversation.

1. **Paraphrase before judging**: rewrite in your own words, in three to five lines, what the document proposes. If you cannot, it is a finding: the document cannot be understood.
2. **Terms**: every term, acronym or system cited must be defined in the document. An undefined one is a finding, even if you know it.
3. **Assumptions**: list what the document takes for granted. Each undeclared one is a candidate finding.
4. **The missing step**: where the reasoning jumps from A to C, name the B that is missing.
5. **One instruction, one interpretation**: a sentence that two readers can carry out in two different ways is a finding.
